### 2.4 Multi-Defect Penalty & Final Score Clamping
* **Source Location:** `ml/inference/severity.py`, lines 123–125:

* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Bonus} = \min\left(1.2, \, (N - 1) \times 0.4\right)$$
  $$S_{\text{final}} = \min\left(10.0, \, S_{\text{base}} + \text{Bonus}\right)$$
* **Behavior:**
  * $N = 1$: Bonus $= 0.0$
  * $N = 2$: Bonus $= +0.4$
  * $N = 3$: Bonus $= +0.8$
  * $N \ge 4$: Bonus $= +1.2$ (saturated cap)
  * Final score is strictly capped at $10.0$.
